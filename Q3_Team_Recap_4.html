Where We Excelled and Where We Fell Behind

Excelled

  • Nearly 3 points of square foot occupancy in a single quarter
  • Rent roll up more than $100K a month
  • Average move in rent up 7.8% while move in volume held at 6,247
  • Lead conversion jumped from 59% to 68%
  • Mission Control went live 9/17, the sign audit covered 161 stores, and SWEEP photos went from half to 93%
  • Seven onboards absorbed while the existing book grew

Fell Behind

  • Move outs up 17% (5,671 vs 4,867), cutting net absorption to 576 units vs 1,313 in Q2
  • Lead volume down 24% quarter over quarter; conversion carried us
  • Unrentables did not get the cleanup they needed. Most of the movement in Q3 was one new building released after its CO, not units returned to rent. We start Q4 at 677 on the Unrent Intel tab
  • Chevis (+0.7 pts) and Ernie (down 0.5 pts) did not move. Ernie's district carries the team's highest occupancy and absorbed the Booneville fire, but flat is not the goal

Q4 Focus: The Next 90 Days

Q4 is seasonally our softest quarter. The goal is to protect what we built in Q3 and finish the year clean. Targets run through 12/31 on the same 166 site base.

Square foot occupancy at or above 74.5%. Hold the Q3 gain through the slow season.
Unrentables below 550 on Unrent Intel, down from 677 on 10/4. Start with the 134 cleaning required units. Chevis, Ernie and Joey carry the biggest counts.
Positive net move ins every month. Know your move out reasons and act on them.
Lead conversion at or above 65%.
Owner calls 100% DM led, with the owner report reviewed before every call.
Budget Camp 2027: every site budget reviewed and approved by 11/30.
Ben fully onboarded and the Storage Depot realignment complete, including the SOT.
Clean onboards: every new site set up right in Cubby, Basecamp and the SOT from day one.
